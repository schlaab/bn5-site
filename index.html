Inbox-bridge-mcp

A private, single-operator tool for reading your own Gmail and Google Calendar across several Google accounts.

What Inbox-bridge-mcp does

Inbox-bridge-mcp is a small program that runs on its operator's own computer. Its operator has several Google accounts (personal and business). Inbox-bridge-mcp connects to each of those accounts, with the account owner's permission, so the operator can see in one place:

  • unread email and the messages that need a reply, across all of the accounts;
  • today's and tomorrow's calendar events, across all of the accounts;
  • the full text of a specific email thread, and its attachments, when the operator asks for them.

It uses this to produce a daily summary (a "morning briefing") of the operator's own mail and calendar. On the two accounts where the operator enables it, it can also apply labels, mark messages as read and save a reply as a Gmail draft for the operator to review. Inbox-bridge-mcp never sends email, never deletes email and never changes calendar events.

Who can use it

Inbox-bridge-mcp is not a public service and has no sign-up. It is used only by its operator, with Google accounts that the operator owns or whose owners have explicitly authorized it. Each account owner signs in with Google and can revoke access at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google user data

Inbox-bridge-mcp requests read access to Gmail and Google Calendar, and on two of the operator's own accounts the Gmail "modify" permission for labels, read state and drafts. Data stays on the operator's computer; nothing is stored on any server run by Inbox-bridge-mcp. Inbox-bridge-mcp's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Full details are in the Inbox-bridge-mcp privacy policy.
